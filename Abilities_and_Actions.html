Abilities and Actions

The two enums and the five nodes that let you pick which mechanics exist and read what the hero is doing.

Abilities (Corridor Ability)

One entry per switchable mechanic. Each is a checkbox under Corridor | Abilities in the Details panel of the Corridor Character (and, for the seven your own character can honour, under 5. Abilities on the Corridor Hero component). All are on by default.

Value Tooltip
Double Jump The second jump in mid-air. The ground jump, coyote time, jump buffer and crate bounce always work.
Spin The spin attack on the ground, in the air and out of a slide, its mash chain and the triple spin. The Cyclone power spins without it.
Slide Slide, crouch / crawl, the slide-spin burst and the high jump out of a slide or crouch.
Slam The slam (belly flop): the slide button in the air - hang, drop, flatten, rebound.
Wall Run Catching a Corridor Wall Run, running along it and the wall leap. Off: the wall is just a wall.
Rope Swing Grabbing a Corridor Rope Swing, climbing it and the rope leap. Off: the rope is scenery.
Grind Rail Landing on and riding a Corridor Grind Rail, rail hop, transfer and hanging under the rail. Off: the hero passes the rail.
Glide The Glide power's effect (jump held while falling). Needs the Glide power worn, so Powers must be on too.
Mounts Boarding and riding a Corridor Mount or Corridor Jetboard (ride, boost, mount jump). Corridor Character only.
Powers Wearing and switching on any power (Phase, Cyclone, Time Slow, Gravity, Glide) from a shrine, Equip or the power button. Corridor Character only.
Shield The shield companion: absorbs a lethal hit, auto-granted after repeated deaths. Corridor Character only.

Actions (Corridor Action)

What the hero is doing now, one value per frame. Highest priority first: Dead, Mount, Rail Hang, Grind Rail, Wall Run, Rope Swing, Cyclone, Spin, Slide, Slam, Crouch, Glide, Double Jump, None.

Value Meaning
None None of the kit's actions: standing, running, jumping or falling.
Dead Dead, until the respawn.
Mount On a mount or jetboard (boarding or riding).
Grind Rail Riding a grind rail (ride, rail hop, transfer, climbing back up).
Rail Hang Hanging under a grind rail (or dropping into the hang).
Wall Run Running along a wall run.
Rope Swing On a rope swing (hanging, climbing, sliding down).
Cyclone The Cyclone power is on (a spin that lasts).
Spin A spin attack is running.
Slide Sliding.
Slam Slamming: the hang and the drop of a belly flop, until landing.
Crouch Crouching or crawling (not sliding).
Glide Gliding with the Glide power.
Double Jump Rising from the double jump, until the apex.

Nodes

Node On What it does
Set Ability Enabled (Ability, Enabled) Corridor Character, Corridor Hero component Switches one ability on or off (the same checkbox as the Details box). Switching a running move off ends it cleanly: the hero drops off a wall / rope / rail, a slide or slam stops, a mount lets go, the worn power is taken away, the shield is removed. On your own character Glide, Mounts, Powers and Shield exist only on a Corridor Character.
Is Ability Enabled (Ability) both True if the ability is on. On your own character Glide, Mounts, Powers and Shield report false.
Get Corridor Action both What the hero is doing now, updated once per frame. It needs no Anim Set.
On Action Started (Action) both An action began. Fires after the previous action's On Action Ended, at most one frame late. Bind it on the Corridor Hero component on any route: the Corridor Character fires it through the component too.
On Action Ended (Action) both An action finished. Every On Action Started gets exactly one of these.

See the recipe for your own animation and the full node list in the Blueprint API by topic.
