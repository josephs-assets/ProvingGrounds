This inventory records the current internal build. It is not a final distribution clearance.

Original production

  • The sound effects (Workshop set and Lab set) and the Workshop music loop are locally synthesized, sample-free work documented in the production sound manifest. The three Lab music loops (MUS_Lab_Course, MUS_Lab_Boss, MUS_Lab_Menu) are original compositions rendered with the instruments of the FluidR3_GM SoundFont (see "SoundFont instruments"). No external sound pack or voice recording is included.
  • Workshop box, pickup, enemy and modular-kit geometry is generated by the original production builders. The 75 enemy clips use the original rig and animation builders.

  • The Meadow terrain layout, turf shelves and path stones are original; its scanned rocks, foliage and material sources are listed below.

  • The 44 Workshop hero action clips were authored for the project. Runtime feedback includes 24 original effects and three analytic sprite materials.

UI fonts

The UI embeds Geist 600 for labels, Geist Mono 500 for menu numbers and Instrument Serif Italic for signatures. The in-play HUD (v3) adds static instances of Geist Black 900 (counter numerals), Geist ExtraBold 800 (the lives sign and keycap letters) and Geist Mono Bold 700 (secondary numbers and the time-trial clock), cut from the variable Geist and Geist Mono fonts with fontTools; the instances keep the SIL Open Font License 1.1. The supplied license texts are Geist and Geist Mono and Instrument Serif.

SoundFont instruments

The three Lab music loops were rendered with the FluidR3_GM SoundFont, Copyright 2000-2002, 2008 Frank Wen, released under the MIT licence. Only the rendered audio ships; the SoundFont itself is not included. Licence text: Licenses/MIT-FluidR3_GM.txt.

Epic template content

The mannequin meshes, skeleton, animation assets and default animation blueprint correspond to 45 paths in the installed Unreal Engine 5.7 High Characters template resources. They were relocated/resaved and are not byte-identical to the template files. Prototyping content and Engine-provided lighting/sky resources are also used. These assets are Epic Games content, not original product art or CC0.

Epic content is included for use in Unreal Engine projects under the Unreal Engine EULA, as Epic permits for content distributed on Fab.

Scope of the notice

Development fixtures and reference-inspection tools are excluded from the delivery copy. The source inventory is retained with the authoring records. Final licence verification, unused-content review and human visual acceptance remain open; no release-ready claim follows from this notice.
