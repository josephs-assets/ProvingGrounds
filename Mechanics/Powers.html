1. What it does

One power is worn at a time; the power button (Q / Y) switches it on and off. Give a power with a Corridor Power Shrine, take it with a Corridor Power Remover.

Power Effect
Phase Swaps which objects are solid: actors with a Corridor Phase component (or Corridor Phase Block) are solid in their default phase and a see-through ghost in the other.
Cyclone Endless spin, light gravity, its own two jumps, breaks strong crates, sets off fuse boxes on touch.
Time Slow Slows the world, not the hero (3 s, world scale 0.35).
Gravity Flips gravity: walk on the ceiling.
Glide Hold Jump on the way down to glide. See Glide.

2. Turn on / off

  • Details: Corridor | Abilities > Can Use Powers.
  • Blueprint: Set Ability Enabled (Ability = Powers) / Is Ability Enabled.
  • Routes: B only (Corridor Character). Powers need the Corridor Character: on Option A they do not work (the power component logs a warning in the Output Log). Option C: no.
  • Off: shrines stay hidden, Equip does nothing, the power button does nothing. Switched off while a power is on: the power is taken away and speed and gravity go back to normal.
  • Individual powers have no switch of their own except Glide. To limit which powers exist, place only those shrines or call Equip yourself.

3. Level actor to place

Corridor Power Shrine (set Power), Corridor Power Remover, Corridor Phase Block (or any actor plus a Corridor Phase component). A checkpoint crate can hand the power back after a death.

5. Blueprint functions and events

Node Kind What it does
Set Ability Enabled / Is Ability Enabled Function / Getter The switch above (Ability = Powers).
Get Powers (hero) Getter The power component. Then:
Equip, Unequip, Toggle, Power Off Functions Wear, remove, switch on / off.
Get Equipped, Is Power On, Is Power Active Getters The state.
Get Time Slow Left, Get Cooldown Left, Get Glide Left, Get Power Duration, Get Cooldown Duration, Get Air Flips Getters Timers for your own HUD.
Get Corridor Action Getter Cyclone or Glide while those are on. Phase, Time Slow and Gravity have no action value (they change the world, not the pose); read Is Power Active.
On Power Pressed Function Call it from your own input.

6. Use your own animation

Leave Anim Set empty; Get Corridor Action = Cyclone / Glide; for the other three read Is Power Active.

7. See it in the demo

Course3_Powers: one shrine per power with a short test area each. System_Gallery shows the five side by side.

8. Pitfalls

  • Only power-ups (everything else off, Powers on): the hero still runs, ground-jumps and bounces off crates; Cyclone still spins and double-hops because those are its own. See Recipe: only power-ups.
  • Sound and effect keys for powers never play when the ability is off.
  • To clear a power from Blueprint use Unequip; do not reset flags yourself.

Power details from the guide

ECorridorPower: one is worn at a time, the power button (Q / Y) switches its power on and off.

Power Effect
Phase Swaps which objects are solid: actors with a UCorridorPhaseComponent (or ACorridorPhaseBlock) are solid in their DefaultPhase and a see-through ghost in the other.
Cyclone Endless spin, light gravity, own jump, breaks strong crates, sets off fuse box on touch.
TimeSlow Slows the world, not the hero (TimeSlowDuration 3 s, TimeSlowWorldScale 0.35).
Gravity Flips gravity: walk on the ceiling (MaxGravityFlipsInAir limits flips in the air).
Glide While it is on, holding Jump on the way down glides: the fall is held at GlideMaxFallSpeed (220 cm/s; a faster fall brakes down at GlideBrake), gravity x GlideGravityScale (0.35), air control unchanged; letting go of Jump falls normally. On for GlideDuration (6 s, 0 = until switched off), then GlideCooldown. Sound / effect keys PowerOn_Glide, PowerOff_Glide, PowerEquip_Glide, ... as the other powers, plus GlideLoop while gliding; the anim set's GlideLoop (empty = FallLoop). IsGliding, GetGlideLeft read the state.

Give a power with an ACorridorPowerShrine (Power, AppearRadius, AcquireRadius), take it with an ACorridorPowerRemover. A checkpoint can hand the power back after a death (ForcedPower / CheckpointPower). From Blueprint: hero GetPowers then Equip, Unequip, Toggle, PowerOff, GetEquipped, IsPowerOn. Tuning values live on UCorridorPowerComponent and ACorridorCharacter (Cyclone..., Glide..., GravityFlipSpeedScale).
