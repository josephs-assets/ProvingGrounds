Crates, bounce and ground jump

This is the part that is always on and has no switch. It answers the question "what breaks a crate?" when you have turned mechanics off.

1. What it does

The hero can always walk, jump from the ground (with coyote time and a jump buffer) and land on crates, which bounce it. Which action breaks which crate decides how your game plays when Spin or Slam are off.

2. Turn on / off

There is no switch. The ground jump, coyote time, jump buffer and crate bounce are core movement.

3. Level actor to place

Corridor Crate (ACorridorCrate); Type picks the behaviour. One actor, 16 types.

4. What breaks what

Crate type What it does
Basic, Surprise Breaks on landing, spin or head-bump; the hero bounces.
Bounce Bounces the hero each time; breaks after a few hits.
Arrow High bounce; breaks only by spin or explosion.
Metal, Metal Arrow Bounces the hero; never breaks.
Fuse Landing lights the fuse; spin or explosion sets it off at once.
Volatile Any touch detonates it.
Strong Bounces the hero; opened only by a slam or an explosion.
Switch Landing, spin or head-bump makes every outline crate real.

The remaining types (Checkpoint, Shield, Big Orb, Time, N-Bounce, Detonator) are described in the README, section 4.

By mechanic: landing always works (and bounces you). A spin, a slide and a slam are the same kind of hit on a crate; the slam also opens Strong crates and flattens only the top crate of a stack. Riding a rail and riding a mount break crates in the hero's path. The Cyclone power breaks strong crates and sets off fuse boxes on touch.

A stack falls when its support breaks.

5. Blueprint functions and events

Node Kind What it does
Is Broken (crate) Getter True once broken.
On Broken, On Exploded (crate) Events Implement in a Blueprint child for your own pop.

6. Use your own animation

Not applicable: jumping and landing are your Anim Blueprint's normal states.

7. See it in the demo

Course1_RunAndJump for the first stacks; System_Gallery for all 16 types in a row.

8. Pitfalls

  • A game with Spin off still breaks crates by landing, slide, slam, rail and mount.
  • Metal, Metal Arrow, Switch and Detonator crates do not count toward the crate gem.
  • Crate bounce counts as the double jump used.
