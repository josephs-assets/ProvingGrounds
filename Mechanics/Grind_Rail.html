Grind Rail

On this page

  • 2. Turn on / off

1. What it does

A spline the hero lands on and rides at 1029 cm/s. Jump hops on the rail, stick sideways plus Jump transfers to a nearby rail, Slide hangs under it, the end launches the hero. The camera follows the bend. Rails catch a walking hero too, not only a falling one.

2. Turn on / off

  • Details: Corridor | Abilities > Can Grind Rail.
  • Blueprint: Set Ability Enabled (Ability = Grind Rail) / Is Ability Enabled.
  • Routes: A only with the kit's movement component or ALS (a rail is scenery on a plain Character Movement) - B yes - C yes.
  • Switched off during a ride: the hero leaves the rail (a transfer in flight is cancelled). Switched off before: the hero passes the rail. "Rails but no hang": untick Hang Enabled on the rail instead; that is a per-rail setting, not an ability.

3. Level actor to place

Corridor Grind Rail (ACorridorGrindRail): draw its spline. Corridor Rail Obstacle (ACorridorRailObstacle): a box on a rail; its Type says what passes it (must jump, must hang, must transfer, ...).

4. Details fields

On the rail (Corridor | Rail):

Field Default What it does
Speed (Speed) 1029 Riding speed along the rail (cm/s). (Details: Corridor | Rail)
End Launch Speed (EndLaunchSpeed) 1800 Launch speed off the end of the rail, forward (cm/s). (Details: Corridor | Rail)
Jump Speed (JumpSpeed) 900 The hop on the rail (upward speed, cm/s), at plain gravity. (Details: Corridor | Rail)
Capture Radius (CaptureRadius) 90 The line this close sideways gets the hero on: against its legs (walking, rising, falling), or under its feet by up to this much while coming down. (Details: Corridor | Rail)
Hang Drop (HangDrop) 82 Hanging: the hero's middle this far under the line (cm). 82 puts the wrists of the hang clip (64 cm over the capsule centre) on a 12 cm rail - measured in PIE; another character's clip may want another value. (Details: Corridor | Rail)
Ride Sink (RideSink) 10 Riding: the hero this much lower than standing on the line (the grind clip lifts the feet ~10 cm - measured in PIE). (Details: Corridor | Rail)
Hang Enabled (bHangEnabled) true Lets the slide button hang the hero under the rail while riding. Off: no hanging on this rail. (Details: Corridor | Rail)
No Hang Near End (NoHangNearEnd) 411 No hanging this close to the end of the rail (cm). (Details: Corridor | Rail)
Transfer Range (TransferRange) 1029 Another rail this close (cm) takes a transfer jump. (Details: Corridor | Rail)
Transfer Time (TransferTime) 0.75 Time a transfer jump to another rail takes (s). (Details: Corridor | Rail)
Rail Camera (bRailCamera) true The camera rides the rail behind the hero (the rail-follow camera: the hero's Rail Camera settings, or this rail's own). (Details: Corridor | Rail)
Override Camera (bOverrideCamera) false Use this rail's own rail-follow camera (Camera Settings) instead of the hero's (Camera Director > Rail Camera). Needs Rail Camera on. (Details: Corridor | Rail)
Camera Settings (CameraSettings) - This rail's rail-follow camera, used with Override Camera. (Details: Corridor | Rail)
Curve Lean Radius (CurveLeanRadius) 600 Riding a curve this tight (radius, cm) leans the hero fully into it; wider curves lean less. 0 = no lean from curves (the stick still leans). (Details: Corridor | Rail)
Curve Bank Angle (CurveBankAngle) 12 Degrees the body banks into a curve at full lean (riding only; it eases out near the rail's end). 0 = stays upright. (Details: Corridor | Rail)
Tightest Curve Radius (TightestCurveRadius) read only Measured from the line: the tightest curve's radius seen from above (cm; 0 = straight). (Details: Corridor | Rail)
Peak Turn Rate (PeakTurnRate) read only Measured from the line: how fast the heading turns there at Speed (deg/s). Over 150 the rail camera's Max Turn Rate holds the view back on that curve; widen the curve or lower Speed. (Details: Corridor | Rail)
Both Ways (bBothWays) false Off: always rides the way the line is drawn (the obstacles are laid out for one direction). On: rides the way the hero was going along it when it got on. (Details: Corridor | Rail)
Rail Radius (RailRadius) 12 Thickness of the rail tube (radius, cm); the line is its top. (Details: Corridor | Rail)

On the hero (Corridor | Traversal): Rail Hit Extent, Rail Hit Up, Traversal Fx.

5. Blueprint functions and events

Node Kind What it does
Set Ability Enabled / Is Ability Enabled Function / Getter The switch above (Ability = Grind Rail).
Get Corridor Action Getter Grind Rail while riding (ride, hop, transfer, climbing back up), Rail Hang while hanging under it.
On Action Started / Ended Event dispatchers One pair per ride; Rail Hang is its own pair.
Get Rail State (movement component) Getter Ride, hop, transfer, hang ...
Get Rail Ride Lean / Get Rail Curve Lean / Get Rail Bank (movement component) Getters The lean and bank of the body, for your own pose.
Get Rail Lean (movement component) Getter The stick's lean alone, -1 left to 1 right.
Get Hang Blend (movement component) Getter 0 riding on top to 1 hanging under the rail.
Get Rail Swing (movement component) Getter Degrees the body swings about the rail while hanging.

6. Use your own animation

Leave Anim Set empty; Get Corridor Action = Grind Rail / Rail Hang; blend ride and hang with Get Hang Blend, lean with Get Rail Lean, swing with Get Rail Swing.

7. See it in the demo

Course4_RideChase: the curvy rails at the start. Walk or jump onto the first rail.

8. Pitfalls

  • A level whose only way over a pit is a rail is impassable when Grind Rail is off.
  • Rail obstacles only affect a hero that is on the rail.
  • The rail camera engages only while riding; camera views you placed "above / below" the rail are only reached then.
  • Riding a rail breaks crates in the hero's path.
