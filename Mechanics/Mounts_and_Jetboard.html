Mounts and Jetboard

1. What it does

Walk near a Corridor Mount and the hero rides it along its path: the stick steers, Jump is the mount's jump, Spin is the boost. A Corridor Jetboard is a board ridden freely on Corridor Water. Ramps throw the mount into the air; stat gates change its speed mid-run.

2. Turn on / off

  • Details: Corridor | Abilities > Can Use Mounts.
  • Blueprint: Set Ability Enabled (Ability = Mounts) / Is Ability Enabled.
  • Routes: B only (Corridor Character).
  • Off: mounts wait and never take the hero (and a hero is not put back on a mount after a respawn). Switched off while riding: the hero lets go and can move.

3. Level actor to place

Corridor Mount, Corridor Jetboard (with Corridor Water and Corridor Whirlpool), Corridor Mount Ramp, Corridor Mount Stat Gate. Draw the mount's Path; for the jetboard put the end of the Path (the finish line) Dismount Distance before the dock.

Jetboard (ACorridorJetboard): Coast Speed, Boost Speed, Dismount Distance, Ride Height, Rotation Rate - see the tooltips in Details.

6. Use your own animation

Leave Anim Set empty; Get Corridor Action = Mount; the mount's own getters (Get Speed, Is Boosting; jetboard Get Turn, Get Drive Level) drive the blend.

7. See it in the demo

Course4_RideChase: the ride sections. Walk up to the mount.

8. Pitfalls

  • Turn Mounts on before the level starts (or after a respawn): a mount that has ended is not re-armed when you switch the ability on later in the same run.
  • Option B only: mounts need the Corridor Character.
  • While riding, the spin button is the boost and Jump is the mount's jump. That is still true with Spin and Double Jump off.
  • A mount must be ended with End Ride; do not just clear flags.

More about mounts and the jetboard

Actor Use
ACorridorMount Walk within MountDistance and the hero rides it along Path: stick steers, jump and spin (boost) are the mount's. The hero sits on it with the anim set's Vehicle\|Mount clips: MountRide (loop; empty = Idle, never the run cycle) blending into MountRideFast (empty = MountRide) as a boost takes the mount from its Speed to Speed x BoostPeak.
ACorridorJetboard A board ridden freely on ACorridorWater: the stick is where it goes (RotationRate; Speed at full stick, CoastSpeed with none), holding spin boosts (BoostSpeed, at least BoostMinTime), jump only if bCanJump. Past the end of Path (the finish line) the hero hops DismountDistance ahead onto the dock: put Path's end that far before it. Mount ramps and checkpoints work as with ACorridorMount; walls stop it (BlockedTime 0). Put a camera view with the Vehicle preset over the water. A skeletal board: set BoardMesh + BoardAnims (or the theme's JetboardLook); it rides under the hero's feet and plays the same blend; nobody on it, it plays its set's Idle. Placed over water, it waits at RideHeight above the surface. The hero's anim set fills Vehicle\|Jetboard: a 3 x 3 blend (BoardSlow / BoardRide / BoardBoost rows by speed, centre / left / right by turn, BoardLeanSample, BoardSmoothTime), the air clips BoardJumpStart, BoardApex, BoardFall, BoardLand and deaths on the board BoardDeaths. Empty slots are skipped.
